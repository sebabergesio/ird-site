02 / What happens next

Three steps.
No maze.

There is no funnel and no auto-reply. The first filter is whether the problem can be documented and whether there is enough data to do it.

01 · Response

I read and reply

02 · Consultation

One hour to organize

If there is a fit, we have a one-hour video call (USD 80 ). We define scope, alternatives and constraints.

Proposal or exit

If the project can be documented, you receive a proposal with price and timeline. If not, I tell you why and what you would need.

The consultation is credited toward the project if you move forward. There is no charge for replying to a message or for saying no.
