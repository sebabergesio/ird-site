02 / The problem

Knowing whether it is possible,
before investing, tendering or executing.

You have a problem or an idea. You need to know whether it is technically possible, with which assumptions, which limits and what other solutions exist, before investing, tendering or executing.

IRD documents that answer: a technical feasibility conclusion, with assumptions and limits. The decision is yours: you decide, approve and execute, with your team or with a qualified professional.

03 / Method

Seven steps.
No endless meetings.

The level of technical development is tailored: it is defined by what the client requests. The process is always the same, and every step leaves something in writing.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    Problem or idea: data, constraints, what has been tried and what is missing.

  2. 02

    Technical analysis

    Criteria, calculations and applicable standards. What conditions the solution.

  3. 03

    Other possible solutions

    Alternatives to the same problem, compared using explicit criteria.

  4. 04

    Feasibility conclusion

    Whether it is technically possible, with declared assumptions and limits.

  5. 05

    Document delivery

    Final PDF, in Spanish or English.

  6. 06

    Review and approval

    By the client. Two revision rounds and a handover call.

  7. 07

    Execution

    By the client, with their team or with a qualified professional.

Timelines and scope are confirmed in the quote.

05 / What you get

A document
to decide and execute.

The document is organized into 8 fixed sections. This is what you receive, at every scope level.

  • The problem and its root cause.
  • Other possible solutions to the same problem.
  • The developed solution: design criteria and schematics.
  • The calculations and assumptions.
  • The limits of the scope.
  • The technical feasibility conclusion.
  • The next steps.
Document scope: No cost estimates, no profitability analysis, no economic comparison and no execution plan, unless the client requests it separately.

07 / Service limits

How far
IRD goes.

IRD does not sign as a licensed engineer, does not execute works, does not direct works, does not manufacture equipment, does not make physical prototypes for clients, and does not guarantee third-party results. Professional validation and sign-off are performed by the client's licensed professional or another qualified third party.

08 / Questions

What people
usually ask.

If your question is not here, write to me. I reply within 24–48 business hours.

What do you need from me?

Data about the problem, access to available information, one person responsible on your side and 2–3 hours of interviews. If a critical piece of data is missing, I tell you before quoting and we define how to obtain it.

How long does it take?

It depends on complexity and urgency. As a reference, the express diagnosis takes 7–10 days and the full document takes 15–25 days from the moment I receive the data and the 50% advance. The timeline is confirmed in the quote.

How do I pay?

Prices are shown in USD and in ARS at the day's MEP rate + 5%, with a live exchange rate. The USD price is the anchor. 50% in advance and 50% on delivery. The quote is valid for 7 days. Unpaid balances are adjusted by IPC. For international clients, we coordinate the payment method when the project is confirmed.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I deliver documents in Spanish and English and hold calls and support in Spanish, remotely. Prices are shown in USD and ARS; the international payment method is coordinated when the project is confirmed.

Does it include costs, profitability or an execution plan?

Not by default. The standard scope concludes technical feasibility, with assumptions and limits. If you need cost estimates, an economic comparison or an execution plan, it is quoted as a separate scope.
