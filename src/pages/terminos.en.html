IRD provides technical documentation services: problem analysis, comparison of alternatives, writing and delivery of a solution document. The scope of each engagement is defined in writing in the quote.

IRD does not execute works, manufacture prototypes, manage construction or sign projects. Validation and professional sign-off are the responsibility of a licensed professional when regulations require it. The document is delivered as technical input for that review.

The client is responsible for providing accurate data and for having the necessary authorizations to share project information.

03 · Quotes and prices

Quotes are issued in writing and include scope, deliverables, timeline and payment method. They are valid for 7 days from issue. There is no fixed price without a written scope.

Published prices are indicative and expressed in USD and ARS. The USD value is the anchor; ARS amounts are calculated at the day's MEP exchange rate + 5%, checked online (source: dolarapi.com). The final price depends on complexity, the quality of the available data and the agreed scope. The initial consultation costs USD 80 and is credited toward the project if the client moves forward.

04 · Payment method and adjustment

In Argentina, USD prices are charged in pesos at the day's MEP exchange rate + 5% (live rate published by dolarapi.com). The quote is valid for 7 days. Unpaid balances are adjusted by the IPC (INDEC) from the quote date until payment.

The standard method is 50% in advance and 50% on delivery. For projects longer than 30 days, 40% / 30% / 30% may be agreed. For international clients, the payment method is coordinated when the project is confirmed.

Late payment is governed by applicable Argentine law. IRD may suspend delivery if the client fails to make the agreed payments.

Scope changes, new data, additional iterations or the evaluation of alternatives that were not in the quote are quoted separately before the work is done.
