02 / Scope

IRD documents.
It does not execute.

I analyze, compare, document and deliver. I don't build prototypes for clients, execute the work, manage construction or sign projects.

The document is designed for a licensed professional to review it, adjust it if needed and sign it when regulations require it. It is also for your team or a shop to use as a basis for execution or tendering.

Important: IRD does not sign projects or replace a licensed engineer. Sebastián Bergesio is a third-year Electromechanical Engineering student at UTN Reconquista. The document is delivered to be validated and signed by a licensed professional when appropriate.

What I do

  • Assess the problem with your data.
  • Compare alternatives using explicit criteria.
  • Document the solution and its limits.
  • Deliver the final document as a PDF.
  • Support validation with your professional.

What I don't do

  • Sign as a licensed engineer.
  • Execute the work or manufacture the equipment.
  • Manage construction or coordinate contractors.
  • Guarantee third-party results.
  • Invent data that does not exist.

What people
usually ask.

If your question is not here, write to me. I reply within 24–48 business hours.

Do you sign the project?

No. IRD delivers the document for a licensed professional to validate and sign when regulations require it. Sebastián Bergesio is a third-year Electromechanical Engineering student at UTN Reconquista and does not replace that signature.

Do you build prototypes or execute the work?

No. In client projects I don't build physical prototypes, execute the work, manage construction or coordinate contractors. The scope is analysis and documentation.

What do you need from me?

Data about the problem, access to available information, one person responsible on your side and 2–3 hours of interviews. If a critical piece of data is missing, I tell you before quoting and we define how to obtain it.

How long does it take?

The express diagnosis takes 7–10 days. The full document takes 15–25 days from the moment I receive the data and the 50% advance. Timelines are confirmed in the quote.

Prices are shown in USD and in ARS at the day's MEP rate + 5%, with a live exchange rate. The USD price is the anchor. 50% in advance and 50% on delivery. The quote is valid for 7 days.
